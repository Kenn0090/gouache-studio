## Gouache Studio 0.98.4

- 3D Paint colour picking: hold **Z** over the model to see the colour about to be picked, then click. Alt only turns the view now. Colour picker sample size (point up to 31×31, averaged) in Preferences; on a mask it picks the mask's grey.
- A lock beside the channel drop-down paints every channel at once.
- Export window: tick single map files on or off; big Export button in the options bar.
- Convert tab: C steps through the maps. Bake tab: C and B step through the baked maps.
- With a mask selected, the layer buttons add paint and fill rows (and filters) to the mask.
- Curvature mask rows: Width, Big/Medium/Small detail, black and white points and a sharpness curve; wide edges follow the surface across seams.
- Scanned grunge now tiles without a visible seam. Paint fades out at grazing angles instead of stopping at a hard edge. Skin height is worked out from the model. Smaller fixes (Save brush in the Brush tab, graphics memory setting, quicker colour changes in Preferences).
