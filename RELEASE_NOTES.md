3D view.

- **View › 3D view (F3)** puts your model beside the canvas. It updates as you paint: the map you're painting updates every frame, and the others a few times a second. Drag the divider to resize.
- **Models:** plane, cube, rounded cube, sphere and cylinder, or **import FBX, OBJ, glTF or GLB**. An imported model is saved inside your .gouache file.
- **Mesh detail** (in Settings) adds triangles, up to 64 times more, so height maps can push the surface out. Imported models are subdivided.
- **Lit** shading uses every map with a sky light and a sun you can move. Normal, roughness, metallic, AO and emissive all show. **Unlit** shows the plain base colour, like most hand-painted games, and is the default for Hand-painted documents.
- **Camera:** drag to turn, right-drag to move, wheel to zoom, double-click to reframe. **Turntable** spins the model.
- **Wireframe** shows the mesh. **UVs** draws the model's UV layout over your 2D canvas, so you can see where each part lands.
- **Settings:** tile repeat for seamless textures, height depth, sun angle and height, light strengths, exposure, lens and background. There is also an option to cut out transparent areas, for leaves and cards.
- **Pop out** moves the 3D view into its own window for a second screen. **Dock**, or closing that window, brings it back.
- FBX files must be binary. Text (ASCII) FBX files are not read; save them as binary FBX, glTF or OBJ.
