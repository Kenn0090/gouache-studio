Selections are here.

- **Marquee** (`M`, `Shift+M` for ellipse), **Lasso** (`L`, `Shift+L` for polygonal) and **Magic wand** (`W`) in the toolbar, with feather, anti-alias, tolerance, contiguous and sample-all-layers options.
- Hold **Shift** to add, **Alt** to subtract, both to intersect. Drag inside a selection to move it; arrow keys nudge it.
- Brushes, the eraser, blend, fills, Delete, and filters now stay inside the selection.
- **Select menu**: All, Deselect, Reselect, Invert, Feather, Expand, Contract, Smooth, Load selection from a layer, mask or channel.
- **Quick mask** (`Q`): paint the selection with any brush, then press `Q` again.
- **Ctrl+click** a layer thumbnail to select its pixels. **Add mask** with a selection keeps just that area visible.
- **Cut, Copy, Copy merged and Paste** (pastes onto a new layer in the same place). `Ctrl+J` puts the selected pixels on a new layer.
- In tile mode, selections wrap across the edges.
