The 3D Paint tab: paint straight onto a model, like Substance Painter.

- **3D Paint tab** (top right): its own textures and its own project file (.gouache3d), separate from your Paint document. Every model gets PBR maps (base colour, roughness, metallic, height, normal) with a base material and a paint layer to start.
- **Layouts:** 3D only, 3D beside the flat texture, or the flat texture only. Colour and Brushes sit in a column beside the viewport.
- **Navigation like Substance Painter** (Alt + left turns, Alt + middle moves, Alt + right zooms), or **3D-Coat style** as a setting. Left drag always paints, no need to switch painting on and off.
- **Hold Alt over the model to pick its colour.** **Left/Right arrow keys** step through the shade strip in the Color panel.
- **Texture sets:** one set of maps per material of the model; click a set to paint it.
- **Mirror X/Y/Z** with movable, snapping planes, and **radial copies**. They also work in Paint's 3D view.
- **Stencils:** paint through a picture (mask) or paint the picture itself onto the model. Hold S and drag to turn, scale or move it.
- **Select parts of the model** by double-clicking: object, material, UV island, face or loop. Painting stays inside the selection, and it can become a layer mask.
- **Materials:** Steel, Gold, Copper, Rust, Rubber, Plastic and more, added as fill layers.
- Fixes: the model no longer locks up in the 3D view (a stroke that never started, or Alt handing the keyboard to the window menu, could stop it turning).
