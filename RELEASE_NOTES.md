The Convert tab: CrazyBump-style map making.

- **Convert tab** (top right): make normal, height, ambient occlusion, curvature, roughness, metallic and a cleaned-up base colour from a photo, a height map or a normal map. Everything previews live on the canvas and on a 3D model.
- **Knows what it's looking at:** the app guesses whether the source is a photo, a height or a normal map, and whether a normal map is DirectX or OpenGL. You can change its guess.
- **Which looks right?** Choose between "bumps out" and "bumps in" previews, like CrazyBump.
- **CrazyBump-style settings:** Intensity, Sharpen, Noise removal, Shape recognition and Fine / Medium / Large / Very large / Huge detail for normal and height. Settings for AO, curvature, roughness, metallic and base colour too.
- **Extras:** straighten a photo taken at an angle, make it seamless, pick colours in the image to set their roughness or make them metal, and mix the new normal with the document's normal.
- **Send to document** (one group per map, optionally replacing the last conversion) or **Export files** as PNGs.
- The Maps menu's converters now open the Convert tab.
