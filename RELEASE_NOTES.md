Fixes for the Convert tab.

- **No more swirls or rings:** normals made from photos and grey images no longer show ripples on gentle slopes (the 256 grey levels of ordinary images are smoothed out first).
- **Better height from normal maps:** the height the Convert tab (and Maps › Height from normal) builds from a normal map is now much more accurate: no rings in the AO and curvature, thin details such as mortar lines come through, and the image edges are no longer raised.
- **Convert shows its own model** again after you've been in the Bake tab.
- **Sent maps are easy to find:** in the layer list, groups sent from Bake or Convert show which map they hold (Nrm, AO, Hgt…), their thumbnails show it, and clicking one switches the view to that map.
