Masks and effects, like Substance Painter: a mask can hold rows of its own, listed under the layer.

- **Mask rows:** paint, white or black fills, baked **mesh maps**, **ID colours**, **direction** (dust on top), **gradients**, **noise** (triplanar, no seams), your own **pictures**, **another layer's mask**, and **generators**: Edge wear, Dirt in cavities, Dust on top, Moss, Rust streaks, Water line, Slime, Crud, Chipped paint, Scratches, Snow on top, Soot, Drips and leaks, Sun-bleached.
- **Filters in masks:** Levels, Blur, Threshold, Posterize, Invert and more, plus Grow/shrink, Warp and Slope blur. A filter changes everything below it.
- Each row has an eye, a blend mode and opacity. Drag rows to reorder them, and ▾ folds them away. Everything stays live.
- **Content effects** (blue fx rows): filters on the layer's own maps.
- **Properties** (the tab beside Colour, formerly Material) shows the selected row's settings, or the material's.
- **✦ button** under the layers adds rows to the mask (mask thumbnail selected) or effects to the layer.
- **Live mask** on a layer without a mask (right-click › Live mask…): ID colours, mesh maps, generators or shapes limit where you paint. **Keep…** makes it a mask stack, or cuts the layer to it.
- **Filter › Mesh maps from material…** makes curvature, cavity AO, edges, creases, roughness and metallic from a material's height or normal. They can go into the mesh maps (a new **Converted** tab in Properties), onto the baked maps, into new layers, or into the material's own roughness and metallic.
- Painting on a mask with rows goes into its Paint row; the eraser takes paint away again.
