## 0.93.4

- **Skew painting fixes show up faster and smoother:** quick preview first, full quality (AO and thickness too) a moment after your last stroke, plus an Edge softness slider.
- **The cage is off by default** and fades in when you move the Front or Back slider.
- **Soften normals** in the Bake tab's Normal map, like Marmoset's.
- **Gouache shaders in Unity, Godot and Unreal:** sending an export into an engine can now add a material per texture set (Standard, Skin, Brushed metal, Velvet, Toon, Cel, Spec/Gloss, Panner, Retro). Unity has URP and Built-in. Unreal uses a setup script; Toon and Cel have their outline in the material.
