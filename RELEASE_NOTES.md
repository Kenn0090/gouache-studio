Bake tab, painting on the model, skew and offset painting.

- **Tabs** at the top right: Paint, Animation and the new **Bake** tab.
- **Bake tab:** the baker's settings sit in the panel, and the model is shown large in the 3D view. You watch the bake fill in on the model as it runs. Bakes stay in the tab until you press **Send to document**, or tick "Send results to layers automatically". "Replace the last baked layers" stops bakes piling up.
- **Fix the bake:** paint **Skew** (black stops details from leaning or smearing) and **Offset** (lighter reaches further, darker less far) on the model or on the canvas. After each stroke the bake updates where you painted. **Estimate offset** gives you a starting point. Both maps are saved with the document.
- **Paint on the model:** press **Paint** in the 3D view's toolbar and paint straight onto the model with the brush, eraser, dodge or burn. The brush is round on screen, only paints what you can see, and crosses UV seams cleanly. Alt+drag turns the model.
