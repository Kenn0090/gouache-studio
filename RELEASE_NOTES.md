## Gouache Studio 0.97.24

- **Wood Edge Wear** follows raised edges and breaks up along the wood grain. Find it under **3D Paint → Generators → Wear and weather**.
- Start with **Rubbed Finish**, **Chipped Finish**, or **Splintered Timber**. Adjust edge growth and distortion, protect recesses, and choose between wear, splinters, or both. Use a bundled wood scan or drop in a custom grain image.

## Gouache Studio 0.97.23

- New Reset view button in the 3D view: refreshes the 3D view from your painting if the shading looks blocky or wrong.
- Grunge inputs are small drop-or-click squares with a gallery, like Marmoset.
- New scratch types (curved, dirty, gouges, cross-hatch, swirl, hairline) with length and thickness.
- Scratches are now extracted from real scans by default.
- Fill black / Fill white clear the whole mask, effects included.
- New material and Reset to default buttons in the material editor.
