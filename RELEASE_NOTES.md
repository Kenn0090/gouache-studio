The looks: real lighting, shaders, ray-traced renders and turntables.

- **HDRI lighting:** the 3D view is lit by a 360° photo of real light, like Substance Painter and Marmoset. Five free HDRIs come with the app (Studio, Photo studio, Cloudy sky, Venice sunset, Evening sky, from Poly Haven), or load your own .hdr or .exr. Turn it, change its brightness, show it as a blurred background, add an extra sun; Filmic tone mapping keeps bright highlights soft. Reflections now follow roughness properly.
- **Shaders per texture set** (Shader panel, a drop-down list): Standard, Skin (light shines through thin parts, using the baked thickness), Brushed metal, Velvet, Toon and Cel (with outlines), and a Spec/Gloss view. Each keeps its own settings.
- **Ray traced mode:** a third shading option in the 3D view: real shadows and bounced light, cleaning up the longer you leave it.
- **Render window:** ray-trace a picture at up to 4K, with the HDRI, a colour or a transparent background, and save it as PNG.
- **Screenshot** button (📷), with a transparent background option.
- **Turntable recorder:** spins, speed, frames per second, size, and WebM or MP4 video, GIF or a PNG sequence; the light can turn with the model or stay still.
- **Bake tab:** show the high-poly (see-through over the low-poly, or on its own); **C** steps through the baked maps on the model (Shift+C back).
- Fixed: some models, like the built-in sphere, were lit from the wrong side in the 3D view.
