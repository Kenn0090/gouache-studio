# Gouache Studio 0.47.2

- Small corner arrows on the painting tools show that more tools are available. Click an arrow or **hold the left mouse button** on an icon to open a menu with icons and names for Brush, Eraser, Blend, Heal, Clone, Dodge and Burn.
- Click a tool to switch, or keep holding, drag onto it and release. A quick click still selects the original tool, and each tool keeps its own settings. The Gradient/Paint bucket group has the same menu.
- Menus work in Paint and 3D Paint, fit left/right and two-column toolbars, and support arrow keys, Enter and Escape.
