Animation mode: flipbooks and sprite sheets.

- Switch **Paint ▾ / Animation** in the top-right corner. Animation mode shows only frames: each frame is its own image, painted with all your usual tools. Your Paint-mode layers wait untouched until you switch back.
- **Timeline** under the canvas: add, duplicate, delete and drag frames to reorder; `,` and `.` step through them, **Enter** plays. Frame rate buttons for **12, 24 and 30 fps** (or type any rate), and a hold per frame.
- **Tags**: Shift+click a range of frames and tag it (idle, run, attack…); each tag loops, plays once or ping-pongs.
- **Onion skin**: earlier frames in red, later ones in green, with how many and how faint.
- **Preview** window that keeps playing while you paint and updates as you go.
- **Import** frames from a sprite sheet (slice by rows × columns), an image sequence, or a GIF.
- **Export** window with a live preview played from the sheet itself: grid presets (Fit, 2×2, 4×4, 8×8, 16×16) or any size, what to do with leftover cells, frame size (100/50/25%), padding, extrusion, power-of-two, tags as rows, and data files for Unity, Godot and Unreal (JSON) or Godot SpriteFrames. Also a PNG sequence and GIF.
- Animations save inside your PSD and come back when you reopen it in Gouache Studio.
