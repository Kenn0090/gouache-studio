New filters.

- **Artistic:** Kuwahara, Watercolour, Charcoal, and Acid (colour flow or glitch trails).
- **Photo:** Soft focus, Cinematic mono and Anaglyph.
- **Print:** Halftone (dots, lines or wavy lines), Engraving (with pop art colour), Riso print, B&W print, and Pixel / bitmap (dithered pixels or 1-bit).
- **Glass:** frosted or ribbed glass seen through, or a glassy overlay of streaks and reflections.
- **Drift blur:** streaky motion blur whose direction wanders.
- **Y2K gradient maps** in Adjust › Gradient map.
- **Tile** has **Uniform scale**: one Tiles slider for both directions.
- All of them are in the **Filter Gallery** (new Photo and Print folders) and work as **filter layers**.
