The Brush tab.

- **Brush tab** (top right, next to Convert): draw brush tips on a canvas of their own, white with a black brush. Your painting stays in Paint exactly as you left it, and the sketch waits here while you switch.
- **Test stroke:** a live stroke painted with the tip as you draw it.
- **Set up the new brush before saving:** spacing, size and angle jitter, scatter, hue and brightness jitter, pressure and follow stroke. Name it and press **Make brush**.
- **Your tips:** edit a saved tip on the canvas and update it, rename or delete it.
- Canvas sizes 256, 512 and 1024, Clear, and centre guides.
