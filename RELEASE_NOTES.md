Panels on a second monitor.

- **⧉ on a floating panel** moves it into a window of its own: drag it to your other monitor. Clicks and shortcut keys there work as in the main window.
- The window's place and size are remembered, and it opens again the next time you start the app.
- Close the window to bring the panel back as a floating panel, or press **⤓** there to put it back in the dock.
