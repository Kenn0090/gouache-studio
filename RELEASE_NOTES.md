## Gouache Studio 0.97.30

- "Hide the bumps below" starts off on new materials; Preferences › Painting can make it start on.
- Flipping through maps with C or B shows each map's name on screen.

## Gouache Studio 0.97.29

- Fixed: dragging the Navigator past its edge no longer breaks the app.
- Curvature detail (Big / Medium / Small) in Mesh maps from a material and the Edge wear filter.

## Gouache Studio 0.97.28

- Curvature detail (Big / Medium / Small) also in the Mask Builder, the Bake mesh maps window, the Convert tab and the Curvature from normal converter.

## Gouache Studio 0.97.27

- Normal mixing (Combine / Replace), AO mixing and UV padding in Texture Set Settings.
- Real skin blur like Marmoset: light spreads under the skin, painted detail stays sharp; Model height for real millimetres.
- Skin goes to Unreal, Unity and Godot with its scatter, translucency and fuzz maps; Specular reflectivity exports as Metallic.
- Lights, Camera and Post FX buttons on the 3D view, each opening a dockable panel; depth of field with sticky focus.
- Easier light gizmo; brush alignment saved in each brush; stroke preview stays pinned.
- Material editor shows only the material's channels and follows the reflectivity; Alt+click a channel button to solo it.
- Drag Projects pictures onto material channels; no zoom limit.
- Heal and clone on the model copy from the surface you see, so seams no longer pull in colour from other UV pieces.
- Curvature detail: Big shapes, Medium shapes and Small details sliders on generators and the Bake tab's curvature.
- Fixed: clone and heal on the model, flicker in the Ray traced view while painting, a leftover wireframe after deleting a layer.

## Gouache Studio 0.97.26

- Paint Scattering, Scatter colour and Translucency for the Skin shader (added when you pick Skin).
- Material slots in the Shader panel: each input is a value, painted, or a picture.
- Specular level channel, and up to 8 channels of your own with names.
- Export each channel as a layered PSD; import a layered PSD into a channel.
- Custom shader maker: mix ingredients from the other shaders (lighting styles, shine, extras), each with a mask channel; a Shaders library on the shelf.
- Material pictures and imported mesh maps in UV-tile sets go into their own tiles (1001, 1002…) instead of stretching.
- Texture Set Settings panel: add or remove channels, 8- or 16-bit per channel; channel buttons on materials and the brush, like Painter.
- Skin shader laid out like Marmoset's: scatter, translucency and fuzz, each with a map; Metalness or Specular reflectivity.
- Focal length in mm for the 3D camera, with common lenses.

## Gouache Studio 0.97.25

- Generator settings in Marmoset-style cards, each picture slot with its own controls.
- More edge controls on the edge presets (strength, growth, sharpness, sharp edges only, corners, distortion, cavity protection).
- A clear Invert button on every generator.

## Gouache Studio 0.97.24

- **Wood Edge Wear** follows raised edges and breaks up along the wood grain. Find it under **3D Paint → Generators → Wear and weather**.
- Start with **Rubbed Finish**, **Chipped Finish**, or **Splintered Timber**. Adjust edge growth and distortion, protect recesses, and choose between wear, splinters, or both. Use a bundled wood scan or drop in a custom grain image.

## Gouache Studio 0.97.23

- New Reset view button in the 3D view: refreshes the 3D view from your painting if the shading looks blocky or wrong.
- Grunge inputs are small drop-or-click squares with a gallery, like Marmoset.
- New scratch types (curved, dirty, gouges, cross-hatch, swirl, hairline) with length and thickness.
- Scratches are now extracted from real scans by default.
- Fill black / Fill white clear the whole mask, effects included.
- New material and Reset to default buttons in the material editor.
