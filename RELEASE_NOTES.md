Colour jitter, your own keys and themes, brushes from the canvas, pictures into masks.

- **Colour jitter:** Hue, Saturation and Brightness jitter in the brush's *Tip shape & dynamics*: every dab gets its own colour, or tick **Once per stroke**. Saved with your brushes.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** (Edit › Keyboard shortcuts…): give any menu command, tool or brush key a new key. **Photoshop keys** in one click, save and load key sets, reset any time. Menus and the hint line show your keys.
- **Shortcut hints:** View › Shortcut hints (or Preferences) hides the line of keys at the bottom of the canvas.
- **Themes** (Edit › Preferences): Dark, **Dark red**, Darker, Warm, Light, or a **Custom** theme from five colours of your own.
- **Make brush tip:** turn the canvas, the selection or a layer into a brush tip, like Photoshop's Define Brush Preset (dark paints). The **Brush tip** template in File › New gives a white canvas and a **Make brush** button.
- **Pictures into masks:** Ctrl+V while editing a mask pastes into it (move and scale, then Enter); drag a layer onto a mask thumbnail; or drop an image file on one (stretched to fit). Works in quick mask too.
- **Filter › Tile:** repeat the image across and down, with row offset, random rotation and random flip per tile.
