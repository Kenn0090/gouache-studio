# Favorites

Open **Window > Favorites**. Drag a toolbar tool or a brush, texture, material, smart material or smart mask from its library into the panel. Drag its tab to float or dock it, and resize it like the other panels.

**2D canvas and 3D Paint keep separate collections**, saved on this computer. Search and the type filter help find an item. Clicking selects a tool or brush, adds a texture layer, or selects a material in its library. Drag texture and material favorites to the layer stack to use them there.

The × beside an item removes only its favorite reference. Original library items stay intact. If an imported item has been removed, its favorite remains marked unavailable until it is reimported or removed.

**Shift+B** swaps between the last two brush presets used in the session, including their remembered sizes. Change this shortcut in Edit > Keyboard shortcuts.
