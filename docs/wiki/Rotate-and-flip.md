# Rotate and flip

**View only** (View menu): turn what you see without changing the picture.

- Alt+, / Alt+. turn the view 15° left or right. Hold **R** and drag to rotate freely at any angle. Add **Shift** to constrain to 15° steps; **Shift + Space** also rotates in 15° steps.
- **Rotate view…** sets an exact angle. **Straighten view** (Alt+0) puts it back.
- **Flip view left-right** (Alt+H) mirrors the display to spot mistakes. Painting still lands where you aim.
- Rulers and guides are hidden while the view is turned.

**Layers** (Edit menu): **Flip layer left-right** (Ctrl+Alt+H) and **top-bottom** (Ctrl+Alt+V) flip the selected layers, or the selection, around the middle of what they hold. Undo works.

The **Move**, **Marquee**, **Lasso** and **Wand** options bar also has horizontal and vertical flip buttons. After flipping selected pixels, the selection fits the visible artwork and the tool switches to **Move**, ready to drag. Undo restores both the artwork and its original selection.

**Whole canvas** (Image menu): rotate 90° either way or 180°, flip left-right (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+H) or top-bottom (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+V). Text, shapes and gradients become pixels, and undo history is cleared.

**Transform warp** (Edit menu, Ctrl+Alt+T) starts a warp straight away. Every key above can be changed in Edit › Keyboard shortcuts.
