# Content and downloads

What comes with the app is the **1K Material Library**, the **1K skies** (lighting pictures) and the grunge maps, so it works right away, even offline. **File › Content…** offers the bigger versions and the AI add-ons in one window, with the real sizes. It also opens by itself once after an update that brings new downloads (the first time you start the desktop app, after the launch and welcome screens). Pick **Not now** and it will not ask again until there is something new.

## What you can choose

- **Material Library pictures: Keep 1K, 2K or 4K.** The bigger pictures come from ambientCG (CC0). They look better up close, but they use much more memory and disk, so most projects do best with 1K or 2K. 2K for the whole Library is a large download (several GB); you can stop at any time and what is done is kept. Press the button again to carry on.
- **Skies: Keep 1K or 2K.** The five skies are fetched from Poly Haven (CC0). The 3D view uses at most 2048 pixels wide, so there is no 4K. **Back to 1K skies** removes them again.
- **AI add-ons.** Tick the parts you want (select subject and sky, remove, lens blur and relight, upscale, height from a photo). The AI engine comes with them. They run on your computer; nothing is sent anywhere.

The web version cannot ask those sites directly, so the Content window only explains that it needs the desktop app.

## Where things are kept

Downloaded pictures and skies are kept inside the app's own storage on your computer. Nothing is written into your project folders.
