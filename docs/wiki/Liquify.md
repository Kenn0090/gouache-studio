# Liquify

Push, pinch, bloat and twirl paint with a brush. Pick the **Liquify** tool (the wavy-lines button after Clone stamp, or Ctrl+Shift+X).

- **Push**: drag to push the paint along with the brush.
- **Pinch** / **Bloat**: hold the button to pull paint in toward the middle, or swell it out.
- **Twirl ↻ / ↺**: hold to swirl the paint clockwise or counter-clockwise.
- **Restore**: paint over a change to softly undo it, within the stroke you are making.
- **Size** and **Strength** are in the bar above the canvas.

Every map of the layer moves together (so a material stays in step), a selection limits where it works, and each stroke is one undo step. Liquify works in Paint, not in the 3D view. Symmetry is not used yet.
